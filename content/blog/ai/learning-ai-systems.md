  Trước khi chọn mô hình, hãy xác định vấn đề cần giải quyết, đầu vào nào có sẵn và câu trả lời tốt trông như thế nào. Nếu chưa định nghĩa được tiêu chí thành công, rất khó biết một thay đổi có cải thiện hệ thống hay không.

When learning AI, it is easy to focus on model architectures or a benchmark score. But a model is only one part of the system a person actually uses.

## Ask the right question

Before choosing a model, define the problem, the available inputs, and what a good answer looks like. Without a success criterion, it is difficult to tell whether a change genuinely improves the system.

## Evaluate with realistic examples

Build a small set of representative examples, including cases that are likely to fail. Track quality, latency, cost, and important failure modes. A polished demo is not a substitute for repeatable evaluation.

## Remember the product

The interface, error handling, privacy, and ability to hand work to a person all shape the experience. AI is useful when it solves the right problem inside a dependable workflow.

I am learning to think of AI as a system of data, models, evaluation, and product decisions—not just a choice to use a larger model.
